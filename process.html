Methodology

Documented process. Verifiable rigor.

Most agencies cannot show you their pre-launch process because they do not have one. Ours has been refined across 17+ audits per week, every week, for years. Every site we touch runs through the same checklist. No exceptions.

Pre-launch phase

Before your site goes live.

Most agencies treat launch day as a deadline to push past. We treat it as a verification milestone. Our pre-launch checklist runs across six categories and dozens of specific items, every one of which gets checked, documented, and signed off before anything goes public. If a check fails, the site doesn't launch. That sounds simple. It's also the opposite of how most launches happen.

Foundation and crawl

Full Screaming Frog crawl of the staging environment. HTTPS verification across every URL. Canonical domain locked correctly (www or non-www, with consistent enforcement). robots.txt configured to allow indexing. XML sitemap generated and submitted. llms.txt created for AI crawlers. Lorem Ipsum scan to catch placeholder content that snuck through. NAP consistency check between site and Google Business Profile.

Tracking and tooling

Google Analytics 4 tagged and verified. Google Search Console connected and showing the live property. Google Tag Manager firing correctly with conversion events tested. Bing Webmaster Tools verified. WhatConverts or call tracking platform wired to forms. Conversion goals tied directly to revenue events, not vanity metrics.

Schema and structure

LocalBusiness schema on every page. BreadcrumbList implemented globally. Service schema validated on each service page. ContactPage schema on the contact page. Blog schema on every post. FAQ schema where applicable. Review schema where validated. Every JSON-LD block runs through Google's Rich Results Test before launch. No exceptions.

On-page and content

One H1 per page, no exceptions. Unique meta titles and descriptions across the site. Image alt attributes complete and descriptive. All images compressed under 200KB. Internal linking returns zero crawl errors. External links nofollow when appropriate and open in new tabs. Open Graph and Twitter card tags populated for every shareable URL.

Local and geographic

City pages have unique content. No templated copy. City H1 structures use city plus service vertical, not generic patterns. City pages linked from main navigation and homepage. Google Business Profile fully optimized and embedded in the footer. Hours of operation match exactly across website, GBP, Yelp, and Facebook. Local citations consistent across at least the major directory platforms.

Conversion and CRO

CTAs placed near solution sections, not just hero. Form titles describe specific value being received. Submit buttons named for outcomes, never "Submit." Spam blockers and bot blockers active on every form. Mobile call CTAs phrased to invite, not pressure. Trust signals visible above the fold. Page speed tested and optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop separately.

Post-launch verification

After your site is live.

The first 48 hours after launch are when most "successful migrations" quietly turn into traffic disasters. We run a separate post-launch verification cycle that catches the issues live deployment introduces. Pre-launch checks staging. Post-launch checks reality. Both are required, never just one.

  • Site using HTTPS confirmed on production
  • Correct www or non-www enforced via 301
  • llms.txt created and accessible
  • robots.txt updated, site indexable
  • XML sitemaps updated for live domain
  • GA4 tagged, verified on production
  • GSC verified, sitemap submitted
  • GTM tags firing on production
  • Bing Webmaster Tools verified
  • WhatConverts setup confirmation
  • Canonical tags resolving correctly
  • No 404 errors on any URL
  • No 301 redirect chains
  • No 404s on internal links
  • No 301s on internal links
  • No 404s on external links
  • No 301s on external outbound links
  • Schema validating on all pages
  • Blog pagination set to nofollow noindex
  • Redirection rules active and tested
  • Image optimization confirmed
  • Alt attributes complete on production
  • OG tags rendering correctly
  • Favicon and Apple Touch Icon working
  • Nofollow attributes applied where appropriate
  • SEO meta data live and unique
