## Methodology

Our pre-launch checklist runs across six categories: Foundation and crawl, Tracking and tooling, Schema and structure, On-page and content, Local and geographic SEO, and Conversion and CRO. Each category contains specific items that must pass before launch.

Foundation and crawl includes pre-launch Screaming Frog crawl, HTTPS verification, canonical domain locked, llms.txt created, robots.txt indexable, XML sitemap submitted, Lorem Ipsum scan, and NAP matches Google Business Profile.

Tracking and tooling includes GA4 tagged and verified, Google Search Console connected, Google Tag Manager firing correctly, Bing Webmaster Tools verified, WhatConverts setup, call tracking validated, conversion events tested, and goals tied to revenue.

Schema and structure includes LocalBusiness on every page, BreadcrumbList global, Service schema on service pages, ContactPage schema on contact, Blog schema on posts, FAQ schema where applicable, Review schema where validated, and JSON-LD validated across all URLs.

On-page and content includes single H1 per page, unique meta titles and descriptions, image alt attributes complete, images compressed under 200KB, internal links return zero errors, external links nofollow plus new tab, Open Graph and Twitter card tags, and HTML sitemap linked from footer.

Local and geographic SEO includes city pages with unique content, city H1s using city plus service vertical, city pages linked from nav and home, GBP fully optimized, GBP embedded in site footer, hours match across all platforms, local citations consistent, and service area markup correct.

Conversion and CRO includes CTAs near solution sections, form titles describe the value, submit buttons specific (not "Submit"), spam and bot blockers active, mobile CTAs phrased to invite (not push), contact form schema implemented, trust signals visible above the fold, and page speed tested mobile-first.

After launch, we run a separate post-launch verification cycle covering 26 specific checks including site using HTTPS confirmed on production, correct www or non-www enforced via 301, llms.txt accessible, robots.txt indexable, XML sitemaps updated for live domain, GA4 tagged and verified on production, GSC verified with sitemap submitted, GTM firing on production, Bing Webmaster Tools verified, WhatConverts confirmed, canonical tags resolving correctly, no 404 errors on any URL, no 301 redirect chains, no 404s on internal links, no 301s on internal links, no 404s on external links, no 301s on external outbound links, schema validating on all pages, blog pagination set to nofollow noindex, redirection rules active and tested, image optimization confirmed, alt attributes complete on production, OG tags rendering correctly, favicon and Apple Touch Icon working, nofollow attributes applied where appropriate, and SEO meta data live and unique.

## Common Patterns We Find

Most US small business sites we audit have the same five problems. Schema is missing or broken (LocalBusiness not implemented, BreadcrumbList missing, Service schema not validating). NAP does not match Google Business Profile (Name, Address, Phone inconsistencies between site and GBP, Yelp, Facebook, BBB). Tracking is half-installed (GA4 fires page views but conversions not tagged, GTM exists but no events firing, call tracking not wired to forms). Forms are converting at half their potential (generic Submit buttons, vague form titles, no spam blockers, mobile CTAs that scare cautious customers). City pages are templated rather than optimized (same paragraph with city name swapped, identical schema across locations, no real differentiation).

Google's March 2024 spam policy update specifically targets templated city pages as scaled content abuse. Most agencies still build sites this way because templating is cheap and looks like volume.

## Approach to AI Search

We optimize for both Google and AI search engines because both matter, and because they reward the same fundamentals in slightly different ways. Schema, structure, authority, page speed, and internal linking remain the foundation. The audience now includes ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini, not just Google.

Generative Engine Optimization is largely just doing technical SEO and content strategy correctly with awareness of how LLMs read and cite the web. The schema work matters more. The entity discipline matters more. The content structure matters more. The fundamentals are the same.

We use AI extensively in our research and editing process. We do not use AI to generate publishable content for clients. Real human writers with real expertise produce the words on finished pages.
