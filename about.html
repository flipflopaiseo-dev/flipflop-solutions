Our story

Built by people
who got tired of
watching you lose.

For 15 years our team worked inside the US digital marketing ecosystem — at agencies, in-house at mid-market companies, and as consultants to Fortune 500 brands. We saw the same thing everywhere: clients paying premium retainers for work that was outsourced, repackaged, and delivered with a branded cover sheet.

The agencies were not bad people. The model was the problem. A model built on information asymmetry — keeping clients confused about what SEO actually involves so they could not question the invoice. A model where AI tools quietly tripled agency profit margins without a single dollar passing back to the client.

We built Flip Flop Solutions to be the agency we wished existed when we were on the client side. Transparent pricing. Direct execution. Results tied to revenue — not vanity dashboards. And now, with AI fundamentally changing what SEO requires, we are the only team in the market that is built specifically for the generative era.
