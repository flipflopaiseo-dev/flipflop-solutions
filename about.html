About

A small team. A documented process.

Flip Flop Solutions exists because most SEO work isn't actually done, and the small businesses paying for it deserve better. We're a methodology-first operations team serving US small businesses with the rigor that bigger agencies promise but rarely deliver.

Why we exist

The agency model is broken.

If you've worked with an SEO agency before, you already know the pattern. You sign a contract. You get a flashy onboarding call. Then the work disappears into a black box. Reports arrive monthly. Numbers move, sometimes up, sometimes down, but the connection between what you're paying for and what's actually being done remains permanently unclear.

This isn't a moral failing on the agency's part. It's the model itself. Most agencies survive on the gap between what clients can verify and what agencies can charge for. When AI tools cut delivery costs by 60% over the last two years, those savings stayed inside the agency. They didn't show up on the client invoice. They didn't reduce the retainer. They quietly expanded the margin.

We built Flip Flop Solutions to be the alternative. We document everything. We show our checklists. We pass AI efficiency back to clients as lower prices instead of pocketing it as margin. We don't lock anyone into long contracts. If we're not adding value in any given month, you should be able to walk away the next month with everything we've built for you. No retention games. No hostage-taking.

That sounds simple. It is. But it's the opposite of how most agencies operate, and that's the entire point. Simple, documented, and honest is rare in this industry. We're betting that's enough of a difference to build a business on.

Our background

Operations at scale, brought down to your scale.

Our team has spent years running SEO operations at scale. Managing rank tracking across hundreds of websites simultaneously. Maintaining hundreds of Google Search Console, Analytics, and Tag Manager accounts daily. Running weekly audit cycles across active client portfolios. Coordinating site migrations across multiple platforms without losing a single percentage point of organic traffic.

That work taught us something most agencies don't admit out loud: SEO at scale is operations, not magic. The agencies that win the most aren't the ones with secret tactics or proprietary tools. They're the ones with documented processes that get followed every single time, on every single site, by every single team member, regardless of who's on vacation or who just got hired.

We took those operational systems and rebuilt them for small businesses. Same methodology. Same rigor. Same checklists. Without the agency overhead, the markup layers, the sales theatre, or the long-term lock-in. What used to cost a Fortune 500 budget now costs what a small business can actually afford.

What we believe

Six principles that shape every engagement.

These aren't aspirational. They're operational. Every client we take on, every page we audit, every report we send, every conversation we have, runs through these six filters. If we ever break one of these, you have grounds to fire us. That's the deal.

You pay for what gets done.

Every deliverable is documented before we start. Every monthly report shows what was completed against the documented plan. No mystery line items. No "strategy fees" with no specific output. If we did it, you see it. If we didn't do it, you don't pay for it.

AI savings pass through.

As our tools get faster, your price goes down, not up. We don't use AI as a margin-expansion strategy. Whatever efficiency we capture from new tools shows up on your invoice as lower prices, not in our pocket as higher profit.

Month-to-month, always.

No 12-month minimums. No early-termination fees. No "phase two" that conveniently appears the moment you ask about results. If we earn the next month, you stay. If we don't, you go. Every month is an audition.

Plain English reporting.

Your monthly report should be readable without an SEO dictionary. Rankings, traffic, leads, citations, conversions. If a number doesn't connect to your business in a way you can immediately understand, we don't report it. Vanity metrics belong in agency decks, not your inbox.

You own everything.

Content, audits, strategies, link reports, schema implementations, technical documentation. Everything we produce is yours on day one. If you leave, you leave with your assets. We do not hold work hostage to extract retention. That practice should be illegal, and at minimum, we refuse to participate in it.

One point of contact.

You won't be passed between account managers, coordinators, junior specialists, and senior strategists. One person owns your account. They know your business. They know your goals. They answer when you reach out. If we ever scale, this principle stays.
