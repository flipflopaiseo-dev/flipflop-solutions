Professional Services

Law firms

Personal injury, family law, criminal defense, estate planning, business law. Legal SEO is one of the most competitive verticals in local search. Schema discipline, content depth, real authorship signals, and trust building are non-negotiable. Most law firm sites we audit fail badly at all four.

Health and Wellness

Health and wellness brands

Chiropractors, dentists, dermatologists, physical therapy, mental health practices, holistic wellness, fitness studios. Health YMYL content is heavily scrutinized by Google. E-E-A-T signals, real practitioner bios with credentials, schema for medical entities matter more here than anywhere else.

Local Retail

Local retail

Independent boutiques, specialty stores, hardware, sporting goods, auto parts, garden centers, gift shops. The local retail playbook is mostly Google Business Profile optimization, accurate inventory data, and reviews. The technical site work is supportive, not primary. We know the difference and budget your effort accordingly.

Hospitality

Restaurants and food

Independent restaurants, cafes, bars, food trucks, catering services, bakeries, specialty food brands. Menu schema, accurate hours, reservation integrations, and review signal management are the levers that move the needle. Most "restaurant SEO" services don't even do these well.

Real Estate

Real estate

Independent agents, small brokerages, property management firms, real estate investors. The IDX integration, neighborhood-page architecture, and listing schema work is highly technical. And it's where most real estate sites bleed traffic without realizing it.

Auto Services

Auto services

Independent auto repair shops, tire stores, oil change, transmission, body shops, detailing, mobile mechanics. Brand-keyword optimization for vehicle makes, service-area schema, emergency intent signals. Auto repair SEO has a lot in common with HVAC SEO. We know both.

Transportation

Limousine and transport

Limousine services, airport transportation, executive car services, party buses, charter buses, wedding transport. Highly seasonal, event-driven, often paired with local hospitality marketing. The booking funnel is short, which means CRO and local SEO compound directly into bookings.
