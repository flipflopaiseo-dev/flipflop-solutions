Accessibility Statement

Our Commitment

Flip Flop Solutions is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level. We believe the internet should be available and accessible to anyone, regardless of technology or ability.

Measures We Take

  • Semantic HTML throughout all pages for proper screen reader interpretation
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios between text and background elements
  • Keyboard-navigable interface for all interactive elements
  • Descriptive alt text for all meaningful images
  • Properly structured headings to convey page hierarchy
  • ARIA labels where necessary to enhance assistive technology support
  • Responsive design that adapts to different screen sizes and zoom levels

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas of our site may not yet be fully optimized. We are actively working to identify and resolve any barriers. If you encounter accessibility issues, we encourage you to contact us so we can address them promptly.

Pencil Trace Feature

Our site includes an interactive pencil trace highlighting feature that allows visitors to mark text passages as they read. This feature is purely visual and decorative. All content remains fully readable without using the trace feature, and the trace functionality does not interfere with screen readers, keyboard navigation, or any standard accessibility tools. If the visual feature causes any difficulty, please contact us for an alternative format.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of our site, please contact us and we will work to provide the information you need through an alternative method.

Standards

This website aims to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more user-friendly for everyone.
